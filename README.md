# Credit Card Risk & Strategy Analysis

## Business Problem
The Credit Card department wants to identify customers at high risk of defaulting
next month, understand the key drivers of that risk, and recommend data-driven
credit limit and collections strategies.

## Dataset
UCI "Default of Credit Card Clients" - 30,000 customers, 23 features.

## Tools
Python · Jupyter · SQL (SQLite) · pandas · scikit-learn · statsmodels · Git/GitHub
